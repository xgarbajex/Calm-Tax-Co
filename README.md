# Calm Tax Co.

Calm Tax Co. is a modern online tax preparation practice focused on making tax filing simple, secure, and stress-free.

Our goal is to provide a straightforward, transparent experience that helps clients confidently complete their taxes from the comfort of home. From the guided intake walkthrough to secure document uploads and electronic filing, every step is designed to reduce stress and simplify the tax preparation process.

## Features

- Guided intake walkthrough
- Personalized document checklist
- Secure document uploads
- Transparent pricing
- Online payment processing
- Electronic filing
- Secure delivery of completed tax returns
- Serving clients in all 50 U.S. states

## About

Calm Tax Co. is an independent tax preparation practice dedicated to providing a more personal, organized, and calming alternative to traditional tax preparation services.
