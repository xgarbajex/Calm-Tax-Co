Client Agreement

Engagement Agreement

Thank you for choosing Calm Tax Co. This agreement outlines the services we provide, your responsibilities as a client, and the terms of our engagement. By approving your quote and submitting payment, you acknowledge and agree to the terms described below.

Scope of Services

Calm Tax Co. provides tax preparation services for federal and state income tax returns.

Services may include individual income tax return preparation, state income tax return preparation, self-employed and freelance tax return preparation, general tax guidance related to the preparation of your return, and electronic filing of completed returns after required authorization is received.

Services do not include audit representation, IRS examination representation, state tax examination representation, appeals representation, legal services, investment advice, financial planning services, or comprehensive tax planning or tax strategy consulting services.

Client Responsibilities

You are responsible for providing complete, accurate, and timely information. This includes all income documents, tax forms received from employers, financial institutions, and government agencies, business income and expense information, dependent information, and life changes that may affect your return.

You are responsible for informing us of any information that may affect your tax return, even if you do not have a supporting tax document. Our work is based entirely on the information you provide.

Document Submission

Documents are submitted through your personalized Calm Tax Co. document upload page. Please upload clear and complete copies whenever possible.

You may upload additional documents and information as requested during the preparation process.

Electronic Communications and Records

You consent to receiving communications, documents, engagement agreements, tax return copies, status updates, and other records electronically through email and other electronic methods used by Calm Tax Co.

Electronic records will be considered the same as paper records for purposes of this engagement. You are responsible for maintaining access to the email address you provide and for reviewing communications sent by Calm Tax Co.

Secure Upload Page Access

Calm Tax Co. provides a personalized document upload page for the submission of tax documents and related information.

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your personalized link and for notifying Calm Tax Co. if you believe unauthorized access has occurred.

Documents uploaded through the secure upload page may be retained as part of the engagement record. After payment is received, previously uploaded documents may no longer be removable through the upload page in order to preserve the integrity of the tax preparation record.

Communication

Calm Tax Co. operates through an online, email-based workflow. Most communication takes place through email, the secure document upload page, and text updates when the client has opted in.

Telephone calls, video meetings, and in-person appointments are not required or generally available as part of the standard service.

Fees and Payment

Each engagement receives an individualized quote based on the complexity of the return and services requested. Preparation work begins after payment has been received.

If additional information significantly changes the scope or complexity of the engagement, an updated quote may be provided before work continues.

Refund Policy

Fees are charged for professional services performed. Once preparation work has begun, fees are generally non-refundable.

If Calm Tax Co. determines that we are unable to complete the engagement, any refund will be evaluated based on the work completed at that time.

Return Review

You will receive access to a copy of your completed return before it is electronically filed. You are responsible for reviewing the return and notifying us of any errors, omissions, or information that appears incorrect.

Please pay particular attention to names and Social Security Numbers, filing status, dependents, income amounts, direct deposit information, and refund or balance due amounts.

Electronic Filing Authorization

Federal and state returns will be electronically filed only after IRS-required authorization has been received from the client.

If Form 8879 or another authorization form is required, Calm Tax Co. must receive the completed authorization before e-filing can occur. Calm Tax Co. retains required authorization records as part of the engagement file.

Accuracy of Information

We rely on information provided by you and do not independently audit or verify the information submitted.

Calm Tax Co. is not responsible for penalties, interest, additional tax assessments, or other consequences resulting from inaccurate, incomplete, or omitted information provided by the client.

Record Retention

You should retain copies of your tax returns and supporting documents for your records. Calm Tax Co. may retain certain engagement records as required by law and professional obligations.

Privacy and Security

Protecting your information is a priority. Client information is used solely for the purpose of providing tax preparation services and complying with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

We do not sell, rent, or share client information for any other purpose.

Right to Decline or Terminate Engagement

Calm Tax Co. reserves the right to decline or discontinue an engagement at any time, including situations involving incomplete information, unreasonable requests, suspected fraud, abusive behavior, or matters outside our area of expertise.

Acceptance

By approving your quote and submitting payment, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Engagement Agreement and authorize Calm Tax Co. to begin preparation of your tax return.

Questions? Contact hello@calmtax.co before approving your quote if anything in this agreement is unclear.
